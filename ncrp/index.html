The Community

Welcome to Northern City Roleplay

Northern City Rolepley (NCRP), is a ER:LC based roleplay community. NCRP has been operating since 2024, and is presented by Corasin Studios.

Whether you're interested in law enforcement, emergency services, dispatch, or something else entirely - there is a place for you at Northern City Roleplay.

Helpful Information

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join a department?

Join the Department Discord via the links above. Most departments require a tryout or department-specific process.

Can I join law enforcement without a rank?

Yes, subject to approved unranked vehicles and the required 3-4 digit numeric callsign format. Please review the Vehicle & Gun Permissions for approved vehicles.

How do I become a certified civilian?

Join the Civilian Operations Discord via the link above and complete the certification application.

How can I appeal a punishment?

Use the In-Game Warn/Kick Appeals resource in the community Discord. For moderation history, please visit - https://melonly.xyz/my/logs

Are staff transfers or fast passes offered?

No. All applicants must complete the normal application process, including applicants from other communities. Departments may allow transfers, this varies per department policy.

How is VIP status awarded?

NCRP Directors may grant VIP status to members who consistently contribute through activity and quality roleplay.

Need more assistance? Visit the community's support channels for questions about department access, permissions, or moderation.
