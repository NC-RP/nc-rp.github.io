PUBLIC RECORDS REQUESTS

What is FOIA?

The Commonwealth of Caledonia's Right-to-Know Law allows citizens the ability to request access to records held by the department.

The Northern City Police Department is committed to maintaining transparency through the release of eligible public records. These records are subject to exemptions, privacy protections, and department policy.

The records request system is currently in development, and will be released soon.

PUBLIC INFORMATION

What Records Can Be Requested?

When implemented, our request system may allow members to request certain department records related to roleplay operations. Available records may include:

Incident Reports

Reports documenting incidents, calls for service, and other activities involving department personnel.

Department Policies

Department procedures, operational guidelines, and other policies governing department activities.

Administrative Records

Certain administrative documents, department announcements, and other records approved for public release.

REQUEST PROCESS

How Will Requests Work?

Once the system is available, community members will be able to submit requests for eligible department records.

Requests will be reviewed by authorized department personnel. Records may be approved, partially released, or withheld depending on department policies, privacy considerations, and the availability of the requested information.

Additional information regarding submission requirements, processing procedures, and response times will be published when the system becomes available.

FEATURE UNDER DEVELOPMENT

FOIA Requests Coming Soon

The Northern City Police Department is currently working on implementing a public records request system for our community.

Online submissions are not available at this time. Please check back for future announcements regarding the launch of this feature.
