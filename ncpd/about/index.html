About The Department

Learn more about the NCPD, our mission, leadership, and divisions working to serve and protect your community.

Our Mission

The Northern City Police Department is committed to preserving public safety, protecting life and property, and enforcing the law with integrity. Operations are conducted with professionalism, discipline, and respect for the community.

Our Vision

We will set the standard for public safety by leading with compassion, professionalism, and creativity. Through forward-thinking strategies and a culture that embraces innovation, collaboration, and enjoyment, we strive to support communities where all can live, work, and thrive.

Traffic Enforcement Unit

The NCPD Traffic Enforcement Division is a specialized patrol division of the Northern City Police Department dedicated to keeping Northern City's roads safe, enforcing traffic laws, and reducing crashes, road accidents, and dangerous driving. TEU officers represent NCPD's frontline presence on the roads.

LEARN MORE →

K9 Unit

As a specialized tactical and search asset, the Northern City Police Department Canine Unit is committed to safeguarding sworn officers, finding hidden suspects, tracking fleeing offenders, and retrieving discarded evidence.
Police service dogs offer sensory and physical capabilities far superior to those of human officers. Utilizing a canine team improves patrol containment, expedites perimeter searches, and lowers risk during searches in restricted or hazardous locations where entry by officers poses an unreasonable threat of ambush.

Marine Unit

The Marine Patrol Division of the Northern City Police Department is dedicated to safeguarding public peace and security across Northern City's waterways. Our mission is to enforce all applicable laws and maintain strict compliance with maritime regulations.

Special Weapons and Tactics

The Northern City Police Department Special Weapons and Tactics unit exists to resolve high-risk law enforcement situations that exceed the capabilities of standard patrol operations. SWAT personnel are trained to operate in environments of extreme danger, ambiguity, and time pressure while maintaining the highest standards of discipline, restraint, and professional conduct.

Office of Professional Standards

The Office of Professional Standards exists to ensure that the Northern City Police Department holds itself to the same standard it demands of the public. PS is not a punitive unit. It is an accountability function. Its purpose is to investigate allegations of misconduct, ensure that disciplinary action is fair, consistent, and evidence-based, and protect both the public and officers from unjust outcomes. The purpose of the Office of Professional Standards is to ensure that standards within NCPD are met as well as setting the professional foundation of our department. We uphold one of the highest forms of responsibility that other officers do not have.

LEARN MORE →

Field Training

The Field Training Officer (FTO) Program is a subdivision of the Northern City Police Department which is dedicated to the recruitment, evaluation, and development of qualified Field Training Officers. Our mission is to prove that any and all cadets can be trained. No matter the reputation, experience level, or even driving level. Our training program is here to accept all and train all who dedicate and serve our department!
