THE PATH TO NCPD

Know The Process

01

Self-Assessment

You will take a self-assessment that you can complete on your own time. You will read and understand our procedures at the NCPD and take a self-paced assessment.

02

Evaluation

Passing your self-assessment will progress you to an evaluation. You will meet with an FTO to apply what you've learn from your assessment onto the training field.

03

Ride-Along

Cadets who pass their assessment will then be required to undergo a ride-along to apply what they've learned onto the streets.

04

Sworn-In Officer

Upon passing your ride-along, cadets will be sworn in via a ceremony or in private and will officially become a sworn officer within the Northern City Police Department.
